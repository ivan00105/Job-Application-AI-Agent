## Improved Version

\subsection{Our Way Forward}

In this project, we develop an integrated platform that automates the entire job application workflow, from extracting structured user profiles from CVs, conducting intelligent semantic job search, and generating tailored CVs and cover letters, to preparing for interviews—reducing application time from hours to minutes. Throughout this process, the platform not only enables users to discover the most suitable job opportunities in the market through semantic understanding, but also helps users identify their strengths and weaknesses, understand key skills required for target positions, and enhance their interview readiness through personalized feedback and preparation.
