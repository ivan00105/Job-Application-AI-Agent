# Introduction Paragraph

The job application process presents significant challenges for millions of job seekers worldwide, characterized by time-consuming manual CV customization, inefficient keyword-based job matching that fails to capture semantic relationships, and lack of personalized interview preparation. Traditional approaches rely on simple keyword matching, which misses relevant opportunities due to terminology variations—for instance, a candidate with "machine learning" experience may be overlooked for positions listed as "ML engineering" or "deep learning," despite representing highly relevant matches. This work addresses these limitations by introducing an integrated Natural Language Processing (NLP) system that automates the job application workflow through semantic understanding, intelligent document processing, and autonomous AI agent capabilities. We demonstrate how modern NLP technologies, including vector embeddings, Large Language Models, and multi-step reasoning agents, can be effectively integrated to create a practical solution that reduces application time from hours to minutes while improving job discovery accuracy and interview preparation quality.
